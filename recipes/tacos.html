Fried tacos recipe

These are my favorite tacos to make mainly do to the seasoning blend I made for them. They taste best on a blackstone but you can make them in any cast iron skillet or just any skillet for that matter. They pair best with some homemade guac or salsa.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp black peper
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 3 tsp tomato/chicken bullion
  • 12-15 white corn tortilas abou small to medium size
  • 2lbs ground beef
  • High smoke point oil
  • 2lbs bag shredded mozzerella cheese

How to Cook

  1. make your seasoning mix using the corn starch and all the listed seasonings above EXCEPT the Tomato chicken bullion
  2. Get your cooking surface nice and hot and use a generous amount of oil to cook the beef in. If using a blackstone only cook on one side. The excess will be used later
  3. cook the beef until it his about 90% done then add seasoning mix to it. The cornstarch in mix will thicken the excess oil and help everything bond to the beef.
  4. once fully cooked add the bouillon and mix well. Wait about 2 minutes more then shut off the heat.
  5. Grab another pan to cook the tacos on or use the clean side of the blackstone.
  6. Lay each tortila flat and spread a handful of shredded mozzerella. Let sit for about 2 minutes and add your beef and close into a taco. Flip about every 2-3 minutes until both sides are nice and crisp.
  7. Plate it up with some guac and salsa and enjoy!

Storage and left overs

These are best reheated in a air fryer or oven even but I have used a microwave and they taste just fine. They last about two days in the fridge but are best if eaten by the next day.

Side Notes

Onions diced are a good add to the beef. Play with the seasoning mixture to your liking and see what works best for you. I will say the bouillon I use is Knorr brand. It Has plenty of salt In my opinion so I didn't even list it as an Ingredient. Like I said I prefer to cook these on a blackstone
