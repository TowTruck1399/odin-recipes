Quick and easy weekday meals.

Every family has a list of meals that are the go to's on a weekly or at least every other week basis. These meals are usually one's that require little to no effort, minimal cleaning, they are affordable and most importantly they're delicious. Sometimes they are family recipes and other times its just something that was stumbled upon looking at a cookbook or recipe list. Whatever it may be the following meals are super easy to make

Korean Beef

The first one is probably by far our most used dish and probably the easiest as its made with mostly household items and quickest so if your looking for fast and filling than this is your ticket.

Korean Beef

Blackstone Tacos

These I call black stone tacos but Ive made them inside on a skillet and they are Just as good. just make sure you use a cast iron skillet for the extra flavor if your not using the Blackstone.

Blackstone Tacos

Mississippi Pot Roast

You can make about a million and one different variations of slow cooker pot roast, some over rice, some over potatoes, some are good to eat just by itself! here is one we go to a lot to switch up our normal roast with rice dishes and it can be prepared in the morning so its ready to eat after work!

Mississippi Pot Roast
